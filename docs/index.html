About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computing Science at the University of Glasgow, supervised by Dr. Ke Yuan and Prof. Iadh Ounis, and a Visiting Research Scholar in the Loscalzo Laboratory at Brigham and Women's Hospital, Harvard Medical School, working with Dr. Hao Xu and Prof. Joseph Loscalzo. Outside of research, I enjoy fishing, hiking, and mountaineering.

Research Interests

  • AI Agents for Scientific Discovery: Agent-native intelligence for multimodal pathology and clinical data analysis, with self-evolving agents learning from expert feedback and execution experience.
  • Computational Biology: Representation learning, interpretable modelling, and benchmark development for biological systems, with a focus on molecular interactions and drug discovery.
  • Scientific Foundation Models: Pre-training, domain adaptation, and post-training of foundation models for molecular and biomedical applications.
