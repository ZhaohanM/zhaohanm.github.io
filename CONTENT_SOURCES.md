6. **FusionGDA**: [publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/25/5/bbae380/7735275) and [Glasgow record](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/328908/) confirm the 2024 Briefings in Bioinformatics publication and authors.

Code, demo, dataset, and project links are transcribed from the CV. They do not imply that interactive demos are continuously available. Journal impact factors and conference rankings are omitted because they are unnecessary for identifying the work and may change.
